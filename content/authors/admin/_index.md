## About Me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the School of Interactive Computing at Georgia Tech, advised by [Professor Humphrey Shi](https://www.humphreyshi.com/home). I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering in 2023 at IIT Roorkee.

Presently, I am a part-time Research Intern at Microsoft Research where I am mentored by [Dr. Jianwei Yang](https://jwyang.github.io/) as part of the Deep Learning Group.

My current research interests revolve around developing multimodal systems. During my undergraduate years, I mainly worked on image segmentation and inpainting. Reach out if you are interested in my research or would like to discuss any ideas.
